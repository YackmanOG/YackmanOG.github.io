Auto Clipper

A private tool that uploads the owner's own Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channels.

What it is

Auto Clipper is a personal program that runs on the owner's own computer. It turns the owner's clips into YouTube Shorts and uploads them to YouTube channels the owner runs.

Who can use it

Nobody else. It is not a public service, there is no sign-up, and it only connects to the owner's own Google accounts.

What it does with your Google account

  • Uploads videos to the connected YouTube channel (youtube.upload).
  • Reads the channel's name, so the program can confirm which channel is connected (youtube.readonly).

That's all. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
